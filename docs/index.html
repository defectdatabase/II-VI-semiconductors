An open defect-thermodynamics library for II–VI semiconductors

DefectDB combines a high-throughput DFT charged-defect dataset for Cd/Zn–S/Se/Te compounds and alloys with machine-learning force fields and an interactive simulation tool. Every formation-energy diagram on this page is computed live in your browser from the published PBEsol dataset — supercell total energies, finite-size corrections, chemical potentials, and band edges included.

InteractiveDefect formation-energy diagrams

Formation energy versus Fermi level, Ef(q, EF) = Edefect,q − Epristine + Σniμi + q(EVBM + EF) + Ecorr, drawn as the lower envelope over charge states q = −2 … +2. Kinks are thermodynamic charge-transition levels. Pick a host, a chemical condition, and any set of defects. The dataset publishes chemical potentials under both Cd-rich and Te-rich conditions for CdTe, and under Te-rich conditions for the CdSexTe1−x alloy hosts.

SimulateThe DefectDB tool

The companion Jupyter tool runs free in the browser on nanoHUB. It bundles PBE- and HSE-fidelity machine-learning force fields (charge states −2 … +2) so you can build and relax your own defects, run molecular dynamics and nudged-elastic-band migration barriers, and construct grain boundaries, dislocation cores, and heterointerfaces — in minutes instead of DFT-days.

1 · Access DFT datasetQuery the charged-defect library, plot formation-energy diagrams, view structures in an interactive 3D MatterViz grid.
2 · Simulate with MLFFBuild any defect in any II–VI supercell, relax with ML force fields, compute formation energies, run finite-temperature MD.
3 · Defect diffusionAutomated migration-path construction and CI-NEB barrier calculations.
4 · GB & dislocationsGrain-boundary and dislocation-core construction and energetics.
5 · CdTe–ZnTe interfaceHeterointerface construction and defect energetics across the junction.

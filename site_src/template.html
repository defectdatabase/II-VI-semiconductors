An open DFT library for chalcogenide semiconductors

DefectDB combines a 6763-compound DFT dataset of I–III–VI2 chalcopyrites and I2–II–IV–VI4 kesterites/stannites with charged-defect formation energies for II–VI hosts (CdTe and CdSexTe1−x alloys, PBEsol and HSE+SOC). Everything on this page is pure DFT — machine-learning force fields live in the nanoHUB simulation tool.

SimulateMLFF simulation tool on nanoHUB

The DefectDB tool builds any defect in any II–VI supercell and relaxes it with our fine-tuned M3GNet force fields — ten models, one per charge state q = −2 … +2 at PBE and HSE fidelity — then computes formation energies, migration barriers (CI-NEB), and finite-temperature MD. MLFF results stay in the tool; this page reports DFT only.
